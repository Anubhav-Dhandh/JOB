You don't have a job problem. You have a funnel problem.

Most freshers send 100 applications with one resume, then wonder why nobody replies. A job search is a funnel: applications, replies, calls, interviews, offers. People drop out at every stage, and each stage needs a different fix.

  • Many applications, almost no replies? Look at targeting and your resume.
  • Replies but no interviews? Look at how you speak on the first call.
  • Interviews but no offers? Look at your project answers and examples.

The book shows you how to measure your own numbers and fix the one stage that leaks.
