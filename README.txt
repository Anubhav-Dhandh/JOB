BEFORE YOU DEPLOY (2 minutes)
1. Open index.html and search for CHECKOUT_URL. Paste your payment link between the quotes.
2. Confirm the price (199) and the refund policy text near the bottom match what you will actually offer.

DEPLOY ON VERCEL
- Drag this whole folder onto vercel.com/new (or run "vercel" in this folder). No build step. Framework preset: Other.
- After deploy, change og:image in index.html to your full URL, e.g. https://YOURSITE.vercel.app/images/cover.webp
